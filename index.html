Weaving Corporate Malaysia into the pulse of the city

Brand and customer experience ideas for Bursa Malaysia at 50, by Chayenne Tan.

Executive summary

Bursa is the heart of Corporate Malaysia. As it turns 50 and sets its 2027–2030 roadmap, the stories of listed companies, and of the Malaysians who build their lives alongside them, deserve to be seen and felt every day: on the city’s screens, in transit, in the MyBURSA app and in Bursa’s own building.

Corporate stories mean little until people see them and feel them. The nine ideas in this board are ranked by how directly they support Bursa’s priorities: quality IPOs, the visibility of listed companies, broader retail participation, and a seamless digital experience. Each is drawn from a review of Bursa and SGX, and from work I have delivered before.

I am committed to being part of the team that takes Corporate Malaysia to its next level, and to leading the change that makes it last.

Relevant experience

Maybank’s station branding at Merdeka MRT — 1 million eyeballs a month

At MRT Corp, I was part of the team that secured Maybank’s station branding at Merdeka MRT. I led the walkabout with Maybank and sealed the deal with the value add of the whole integrated experience, from the station to the linkway.

We delivered 1 million eyeballs every month.

More importantly, Merdeka is not just a station. It is the most symbolic station in Malaysia. It connects Plaza Rakyat to Merdeka 118. Its 180m underground linkway, with sunroofs, connects LRT Plaza Rakyat and MRT Merdeka.

My main point is the symbolism of that connection: it links the people, Rakyat, to independence and liberty, Merdeka. That 180m linkway is a living way — a gallery, and the perfect home for a Hall of Fame celebrating the companies listed on Bursa. It can be art. It can be taken over. Maybank is entitled to that space under its station branding, and the featured companies can co-share the production cost.

This is the model behind ideas 1 and 2: Bursa can lead a full takeover, feature to feature, showcasing all the listed companies, with Bursa as the lead curator of Corporate Malaysia in the pulse of the city.

02 · Ideas for Bursa

Nine ideas, in order of impact

Ordered by how directly each supports Bursa’s stated priorities: quality IPOs, the visibility of listed companies, broader retail participation, and a customer-centric digital experience. Each is a starting point to refine with the team.

Grow the market
01

The Listing Moment: every new IPO, on the city’s biggest screens

IPO PIPELINE

The day a company lists, it appears as the “latest chip on the block”, with its name, stock code and “Powered by Bursa Malaysia”, on the most visible screens in the city: a screen on Bursa’s own building, a branded pedestrian bridge or public space near the exchange, and landmark screens such as Pavilion Bukit Bintang. Kuala Lumpur’s own Times Square moment, every time.

Why it matters

Listing becomes a public celebration that issuers want to be part of, and Bursa is seen every time the market grows.

How it works

Start with spaces Bursa controls, its own building and the area around it, then bundle landmark screens into the listing package with media-owner partners, so Bursa gains the exposure at little cost.

Proof I can do it

Grew MRT’s advertising client base 40%+ and worked closely with media owners on client, agency and media-owner sides.

02

Corporate Malaysia Hall of Fame, always on in transit

LISTED-COMPANY VISIBILITY

Start with the 180m sunroofed underground linkway between LRT Plaza Rakyat and MRT Merdeka, the natural home for this Hall of Fame, then extend to a transit line or flagship stations, with rotating stories of listed companies and the impact they make: jobs created, communities served, products Malaysians use every day.

Why it matters

Makes the value of listed companies tangible to millions of commuters, and builds pride in owning a piece of Corporate Malaysia.

How it works

At Merdeka, Maybank is entitled to the linkway space under its station branding, so Maybank, itself a listed company, can anchor it at no media cost. Featured listed companies co-share the production cost of their own stories. Always on, refreshed with each results season.

Proof I can do it

Led the walkabout that sealed Maybank’s station branding at Merdeka MRT; brought Netflix, Sony Pictures and GrabFood into transit; 2025 OOH awards and an Effie Bronze.

Meet the companies behind the tickers

RETAIL PARTICIPATION

Tours of the real assets behind listed companies and REITs, from malls to plantations, paired with Bursa’s fairs. People invest with more confidence in what they have seen and touched.

Why it matters

Moves events from learning together to belonging, building long-term investors, not speculators.

How it works

Listed companies host; Bursa curates the series and ties sign-ups to MyBURSA.

Proof I can do it

Launched Malaysia’s first digital lifestyle card with a joint bazaar: about 200 guests, key media, half the budget.

06

Bursa’s home as a place people want to be

PLACEMAKING

Brighten the reception and arrival so the energy of the brand comes through, and open a café and co-working floor for companies, investors and partners: a café from a listed Malaysian brand such as Oriental Kopi (or Tealive, once its parent Loob lists), with the co-working run by Colony, Timothy Tiah’s premium workspace brand.

Why it matters

The building is Bursa’s biggest physical touchpoint. It should feel as open and confident as the brand.

How it works

Partner-run café and co-working, programmed with talks, founder meet-ups and listing celebrations, similar in spirit to Asia School of Business.

Proof I can do it

Led Project Collective at MRT to reimagine stations as lifestyle and community hubs, built on commuter research.

07

Bursa Kita: one card for the brands of Corporate Malaysia

ECOSYSTEM

A loyalty card and points programme across Bursa-listed brands, for people and for businesses. Buy from a listed company and earn Saham points; spend them anywhere in the ecosystem. A café card rewards one brand. Bursa Kita rewards Corporate Malaysia.

Members also unlock each other’s spaces at member rates: pickleball courts, HYROX-style training venues, co-working floors and event halls in the townships, malls and REITs of listed developers. Partners fill off-peak capacity; members get a reason to live, work and play within Corporate Malaysia.

Why it matters

Everyday spend and business procurement both flow back to listed companies. When a hospital buys gloves from a listed glove maker, it earns points it can use on listed brands for its staff, from F&B to telco to travel.

How it works

Bursa convenes and sets the rules; listed companies fund the rewards and offer member pricing to each other. Start with employees of listed companies, then open to the public and to SME and corporate buyers. Neutral to every issuer, designed with compliance from day one.

Proof I can do it

Built partner-funded propositions in cards and sponsorships, incl. a 7-product campaign at 5x ROI.

08

The Bursa Media Network: listed media, amplifying the market

LISTED-COMPANY VISIBILITY

Bring Bursa’s listed media groups, such as Media Prima, Star Media Group and Astro, into a network that gives the market’s biggest moments space across their screens, print, radio and digital: Invest Malaysia, the Bursa 50 celebrations and the Bursa event of the year. Alongside it, a shared “Listed on Bursa Malaysia” tag on listed brands’ communications shows Malaysians how Corporate Malaysia lives in the brands they use every day, and what their saham is part of.

Why it matters

Listed media companies grow when the market grows. A network turns that shared interest into everyday visibility for Bursa and every listed company, at little media cost.

How it works

Bursa sets the calendar of flagship moments and a simple tag guideline; media partners contribute inventory in return for content access and recognition. Start with a few willing partners, then make the tag a standard part of the listing package.

Proof I can do it

Worked on client, agency and media-owner sides; grew MRT’s advertising client base 40%+; RM5M+ in PR value at MRT.

A bold idea to explore
09

An art exchange

MULTI-ASSET

Bursa already trades commodities and gold. Art could be a next asset class that attracts new, younger audiences to the brand.

Why it matters

Signals that Bursa’s multi-asset promise is about new opportunities, not just new products.

How it works

Start with a feasibility study and exhibitions with local galleries, before any product decision.

Proof I can do it

Worked with The Artisans Haven and local makers at MRT; launched products that were firsts in Malaysia.
